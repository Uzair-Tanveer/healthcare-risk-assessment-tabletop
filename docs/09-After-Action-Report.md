# 09. After-Action Report

## Purpose
This report summarizes tabletop exercise observations, strengths, gaps, and recommended actions.

## Exercise Summary
A fictional ransomware event disrupted access to systems used by Riverside Family Health Partners. Participants discussed detection, escalation, containment, communications, and recovery decisions.

## Strengths Observed
- Participants understood the seriousness of ransomware.
- Leadership engagement was present.
- The team recognized the need for quick escalation.
- Participants identified the importance of backups and downtime procedures.

## Gaps Observed
- Incident ownership was not fully clear.
- Backup restoration confidence was limited.
- Communication roles were not well defined.
- Patient-facing messaging was not pre-drafted.
- Recovery prioritization lacked written guidance.
- Vendor escalation steps were unclear.

## Key Lessons Learned
1. Backup existence is not enough; restore testing is required.
2. Clear roles and escalation paths are essential.
3. Communications should be planned before an event.
4. Healthcare downtime procedures must be simple and accessible.
5. Leadership needs risk-based decision support during an incident.

## Recommended Actions
- finalize and approve an incident response plan
- assign incident response roles
- test backup restoration quarterly
- create patient and staff communication templates
- document downtime procedures
- improve vendor coordination procedures
- conduct a follow-up tabletop exercise
