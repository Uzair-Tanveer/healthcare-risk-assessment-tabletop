\# 10. Improvement Plan



\## Purpose

This plan converts observations into concrete actions.



| Improvement Item | Gap Addressed | Priority | Owner | Target Date | Success Metric |

|---|---|---:|---|---|---|

| Enforce MFA across all user accounts | Account compromise risk | High | IT Lead | 90 days | 100% MFA adoption |

| Test backup restoration quarterly | Recovery uncertainty | High | IT Manager | 90 days | Successful restore test evidence |

| Formalize incident response roles | Response ambiguity | High | Practice Administrator | 60 days | IR roles documented and approved |

| Create downtime procedures | Care continuity gap | High | Clinical Manager | 90 days | Printed and accessible downtime guide |

| Establish access review process | Excess access risk | Medium | System Admin | 120 days | Quarterly reviews completed |

| Improve email security and awareness | Phishing risk | High | Compliance Officer | 90 days | Training + phishing reporting metrics |

| Document vendor escalation paths | Third-party risk | Medium | Compliance Manager | 180 days | Vendor contact list and procedure |

| Enhance logging and alert review | Detection gap | Medium | Security Lead | 180 days | Centralized alert review process |

| Expand endpoint hardening | Malware spread risk | Medium | IT Support Lead | 180 days | Baseline hardening standard adopted |

| Repeat tabletop exercise | Readiness improvement | Medium | Executive Sponsor | 6–12 months | Completed follow-up exercise |
