\# 02. Business Context



\## Organization Name

\*\*Riverside Family Health Partners\*\*



\## Organization Type

Small outpatient healthcare provider



\## Business Profile

Riverside Family Health Partners is a fictional healthcare organization that provides primary care and telehealth services to local patients.



\## Operational Footprint

\- 1 outpatient clinic

\- 1 small administrative function

\- telehealth support for remote appointments

\- approximately 40–60 employees



\## Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality outpatient care while protecting patient privacy, supporting clinical operations, and maintaining reliable access to care-related information and systems.



\## Core Business Processes

\### 1. Patient Registration and Intake

Collects demographic, insurance, and consent information.



\### 2. Appointment Scheduling

Coordinates provider calendars and patient visits.



\### 3. Clinical Documentation

Clinicians document exams, treatment plans, and follow-up actions in the EHR.



\### 4. Telehealth Services

Supports remote visits for appropriate patients.



\### 5. Prescriptions and Referrals

Communicates care instructions and referral information to external providers and pharmacies.



\### 6. Billing and Claims Processing

Submits insurance claims and manages payments and denials.



\### 7. Records Management

Stores, retrieves, and retains medical records and administrative documentation.



\### 8. Employee Onboarding and Offboarding

Manages access to systems, email, and physical resources.



\### 9. Vendor and Third-Party Management

Coordinates with EHR, billing, backup, and support vendors.



\### 10. Incident Response and Recovery

Supports containment, restoration, and business continuity in the event of a cyber incident.



\## Business Priorities

Leadership would likely prioritize:

\- patient care continuity

\- availability of the EHR and telehealth systems

\- protection of ePHI

\- timely billing and revenue continuity

\- compliance with HIPAA

\- rapid recovery from ransomware or outages



\## Likely Technology Environment

This fictional environment likely includes:

\- an electronic health record platform

\- Microsoft 365 or similar email/collaboration services

\- Windows endpoints

\- identity services such as Active Directory and/or Entra ID

\- remote access or VPN

\- backup and recovery solutions

\- endpoint protection

\- network firewalls

\- printer/scanner devices

\- third-party billing and telehealth tools



\## Assumptions

These are assumptions for the fictional scenario:

\- The organization is small and resource-constrained.

\- It stores and transmits ePHI.

\- It is dependent on digital services for daily operations.

\- It uses third-party services for some operational functions.

\- A ransomware incident would cause major operational disruption.
