\# 03. Asset Inventory



\## Purpose

This inventory identifies the systems, data, and business assets that are most important to Riverside Family Health Partners.



\## Critical Assets



| Asset | Description | Business Importance | Data Sensitivity |

|---|---|---:|---|

| Electronic Health Record (EHR) system | Primary clinical record platform | High | High |

| Microsoft 365/email | Communication and collaboration platform | High | Medium/High |

| Identity system | User authentication and account management | High | High |

| Endpoint devices | Workstations and laptops used by staff | High | Medium |

| Telehealth platform | Remote care delivery | High | High |

| Billing system | Insurance claims and payment processing | High | High |

| Backup systems | Recovery of key systems and files | High | High |

| File shares | Operational documents and shared files | Medium | Medium |

| Firewalls/network security tools | Boundary protection | High | Low/Medium |

| Printers/scanners | Supporting clinic workflows | Medium | Low/Medium |



\## Sensitive Data Types

\- electronic protected health information (ePHI)

\- patient names, addresses, dates of birth, and contact details

\- insurance information

\- billing and payment data

\- employee personnel records

\- login credentials and authentication data

\- vendor and contract information



\## Critical Business Dependencies

\- reliable internet connectivity

\- functioning identity and email systems

\- secure access to the EHR

\- successful backup and restore capability

\- third-party telehealth and billing services



\## Asset Prioritization Notes

The most critical assets are those that support:

1\. patient care delivery

2\. access to clinical information

3\. availability of communications

4\. recovery after an incident

5\. privacy and compliance obligations
