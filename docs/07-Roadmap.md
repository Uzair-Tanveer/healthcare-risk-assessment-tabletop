# 07. Cybersecurity Roadmap

## Purpose
This roadmap prioritizes practical improvements based on risk, effort, and business value.

| Recommendation | Risk Addressed | Business Benefit | Priority | Estimated Effort | Dependencies | Owner | Timeline | Success Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enforce MFA for all users, especially remote and privileged access | R-01, R-03, R-04 | Reduces account compromise risk | High | Medium | Identity system configuration | IT Lead | 0–90 days | 100% MFA coverage |
| Test backup restores and isolate backup environment | R-02, R-05 | Improves ransomware recovery | High | Medium | Backup platform access | IT Manager | 0–90 days | Successful restore test completed |
| Standardize patching schedule and reporting | R-06, R-09 | Reduces exposure to known vulnerabilities | High | Medium | Asset inventory | IT Support Lead | 0–120 days | Patch compliance report available |
| Strengthen phishing awareness and reporting | R-01, R-09 | Lowers likelihood of compromise | High | Low | Training platform | Compliance Manager | 0–90 days | Training completion rate > 95% |
| Improve logging and alert review | R-01, R-02, R-09 | Faster detection and response | Medium | Medium | Logging sources, SIEM access | Security Lead | 0–180 days | Centralized alerting established |
| Formalize incident response procedures | R-02, R-08 | Improves recovery and communication | High | Medium | Leadership approval | Practice Administrator | 0–180 days | IR plan approved and tested |
| Perform vendor risk review | R-07 | Reduces third-party exposure | Medium | Low | Vendor list | Compliance Manager | 0–180 days | Critical vendors reviewed |
| Improve access review process | R-03, R-10 | Reduces excess access risk | Medium | Low | Role definitions | System Admin | 0–180 days | Quarterly access reviews completed |
| Document business continuity procedures | R-08 | Improves downtime readiness | High | Medium | Operational input | Practice Administrator | 0–180 days | Downtime procedures published |
| Expand endpoint hardening standards | R-02, R-09 | Improves resilience | Medium | Medium | Device inventory | IT Support Lead | 3–12 months | Baseline hardening standard adopted |
