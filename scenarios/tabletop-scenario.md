\# Tabletop Exercise Scenario: Ransomware Event



\## Exercise Type

Discussion-based tabletop exercise



\## Fictional Organization

Riverside Family Health Partners



\## Exercise Objective

Test the organization’s ability to detect, respond to, communicate about, and recover from a ransomware incident affecting clinical and administrative operations.



\## Objectives

\- evaluate incident response decision-making

\- test escalation and notification procedures

\- assess backup and recovery assumptions

\- identify communication gaps

\- clarify legal, privacy, and operational considerations

\- improve preparedness for real-world ransomware events



\## Scenario Narrative

A staff member reports that files on a workstation have become inaccessible and a ransom note appears on the screen. Shortly after, additional staff report inability to access shared files and the EHR becomes unavailable. The organization must determine whether this is a localized issue or a broader ransomware incident.



\## Assumptions

\- This is a fictional scenario.

\- No real patient data is involved.

\- The goal is preparedness, not blame.

\- The organization has limited security staff.



\## Participants

\- Practice Administrator

\- IT Lead / System Administrator

\- Compliance Officer

\- Clinical Manager

\- Front Desk Lead

\- Communications Lead

\- Executive Sponsor

\- Legal or privacy advisor, if available

\- External IT/MDR vendor representative, if applicable



\## Exercise Scope

In scope:

\- detection and triage

\- containment decisions

\- communication decisions

\- business continuity decisions

\- recovery prioritization

\- regulatory considerations



Out of scope:

\- technical exploitation

\- live remediation

\- real forensic collection

\- legal advice



\## Scenario Timeline

\- \*\*T0\*\*: suspicious workstation behavior reported

\- \*\*T+15 min\*\*: ransom note appears

\- \*\*T+30 min\*\*: file share access issues reported

\- \*\*T+45 min\*\*: EHR availability affected

\- \*\*T+60 min\*\*: leadership escalation begins

\- \*\*T+90 min\*\*: decision on shutdown / isolation / restore path

\- \*\*T+2 hrs\*\*: communication and recovery planning



\## Discussion Questions

\- Who declares an incident?

\- Who is notified first?

\- Do we disconnect systems?

\- How do we validate scope?

\- What is the backup recovery priority?

\- How are patients informed?

\- Do we contact law enforcement or legal counsel?

\- What records must be preserved?



\## Communication Considerations

\- internal staff notifications

\- leadership updates

\- patient-facing messaging

\- vendor coordination

\- documentation of decisions

\- media handling if escalation occurs



\## Legal and Regulatory Considerations

\- HIPAA breach and privacy implications

\- documentation of suspected exposure

\- obligation to preserve evidence

\- notification thresholds and timelines

\- vendor coordination and business associate responsibilities



\## Recovery Considerations

\- restoring EHR first if backups are valid

\- validating identity services

\- checking for persistence before reconnecting systems

\- prioritizing critical clinical workflows

\- tracking downtime and manual procedures



\## Exercise Success Criteria

\- clear escalation path exists

\- leadership understands responsibilities

\- recovery priorities are defined

\- communications are coordinated

\- gaps are documented for improvement
