\# Participant Roles



\## Practice Administrator

\- Makes operational decisions

\- Coordinates leadership

\- Approves downtime priorities



\## IT Lead / System Administrator

\- Leads technical triage

\- Assesses containment and recovery

\- Coordinates with vendors



\## Compliance Officer

\- Evaluates HIPAA/privacy implications

\- Supports documentation and notification analysis



\## Clinical Manager

\- Represents patient care operations

\- Identifies clinical continuity needs



\## Front Desk Lead

\- Represents intake, scheduling, and patient communication needs



\## Communications Lead

\- Handles internal and external messaging coordination



\## Executive Sponsor

\- Sets risk tolerance and approves major decisions



\## Legal / Privacy Advisor

\- Advises on regulatory and notification considerations



\## External Vendor Representative

\- Supports questions about outsourced systems or recovery
