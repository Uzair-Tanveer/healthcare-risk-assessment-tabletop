# Facilitator Guide

## Purpose
This guide helps lead the ransomware tabletop exercise for Riverside Family Health Partners.

## Facilitator Role
The facilitator should:
- keep discussion focused on decisions
- guide participants through injects
- avoid turning the session into a technical deep dive
- capture observations and gaps
- encourage honest discussion about readiness

## Exercise Flow
1. Introduce the fictional organization
2. Review scope and objectives
3. Present the initial incident
4. Walk through injects in sequence
5. Ask participants to explain decisions
6. Capture observations
7. Summarize gaps and improvement items

## Key Prompts
- Who is responsible for this decision?
- What is the first action?
- What systems are affected?
- What is the business impact?
- What do we need to document?
- What regulatory issue may arise?
- What would we do differently next time?

## Important Notes
- Do not assign blame.
- Focus on process and decision-making.
- Keep the exercise realistic for a small healthcare provider.
- Document gaps in people, process, and technology.

## Expected Outputs
- incident response gaps
- communication gaps
- recovery gaps
- vendor coordination gaps
- recommendations for improvement
